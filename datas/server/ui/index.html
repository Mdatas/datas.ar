Welcome to Datas

Your own AI (Artificial Intelligence) assistant, running on your own computer. Nothing you type leaves this device unless you decide to turn the internet on or use a paid service.

First, one choice: should your AI remember you?

Remember meYour conversations and a short notes file stay on this computer, so the AI gets to know you over time. You can read, edit or delete the notes whenever you want.
Forget everythingNothing is saved. Every conversation starts from zero and leaves no trace — not even technical logs. Fully anonymous.

You can change this any time in Settings.

What will you use your AI for?

Pick one or more. Then press Run: Datas checks your computer and picks the best AI it can carry without slowing everything else down.

Chat & writingQuestions, emails, summaries, ideas, translations.
Understanding picturesDescribe or read a photo, a screenshot, a document.
ProgrammingWrite and fix code.
Doing tasks for meLook things up, read and write files, work step by step (an "agent").
Voice & soundTalk to the AI and hear it answer. Adds a hearing model (whisper.cpp) and a voice (Kokoro); both run on the processor.
Images & video (making them)Datas checks whether this device could do it (it needs a real graphics card). The engine for it comes next.

How much of the computer may it use?

LightKeeps the computer snappy for everything else (video calls, browser, games).
BalancedRecommended. A good AI with a comfortable margin left over.
HeavyGive the AI almost everything. Close other programs first.

My devices working together

If you have more than one computer at home, their memories can add up so a bigger AI fits. Install Datas on each and switch these on. Works on your own network only; speed is limited by the network, so it suits the "Mixture of Experts" models best.
